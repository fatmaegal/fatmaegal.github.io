Bio

I'm an AI Safety Researcher and the founder of JEEL AI, based in Sharjah, UAE. My work sits at the intersection of multilingual NLP, AI evaluation, and responsible AI — asking whether the safety properties we take for granted in English actually hold in Arabic (Modern Standard and dialectal), and other low-resource languages.


Through the PRISM Fellowship I design culturally-aware safety evaluations and analyze where frontier models fail across languages. Alongside research, I care deeply about AI literacy for families — helping parents understand the AI their children are growing up with. Before AI, I spent years in marketing and community building across the UAE, which shaped how I think about communicating complex technology to real people.
