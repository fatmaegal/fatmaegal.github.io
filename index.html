Bio

I'm an AI Safety Researcher and the founder of JEEL AI. My work sits at the intersection of multilingual NLP, AI evaluation, and responsible AI, asking whether the safety properties we take for granted in English actually hold in Arabic (Modern Standard & the 25+ dialects), and other low-resource languages.


Through the PRISM Fellowship, I designed culturally-aware safety evaluations and analyzed where the frontier models fail across languages. Alongside research, I care deeply about AI literacy for families due to personal passion, helping parents understand the AI their children are growing up with. Before AI, I spent years in marketing and community building across the UAE, which shaped how I think about communicating complex technology to real people.


I want to be part of making sure the next generation of language technology actually works for the people it usually forgets.

🧡 Child AI Safety Talk — AI Safety UAE

January 2026 · Coders HQ, UAE · Year of Family 2026

Gave a talk on AI safety for families with AI Safety UAE, as part of the UAE's Year of Family 2026 — bringing 50+ parents, caregivers, educators, and AI practitioners into one room to talk about keeping children safe around AI. Teachers shared how they navigate school policy and home rules; parents asked where to even begin. The biggest takeaway: protecting children from AI isn't a one-person or one-company job — it takes families and schools building it together.
